Industry website planning

A gym website that makes membership and trial decisions easier.

Prospective members usually want to compare facilities, plans, schedules, trainers and location before calling, messaging or requesting a trial.

Discuss Your Website Chat on WhatsApp
Customer needs

Questions the website should answer

  • Understand membership options and what is included
  • Check class times, facilities and trainer information
  • Request a trial session or speak to the gym
Recommended sections

A useful page structure

  • Homepage with membership and trial direction
  • Membership-plan information
  • Class schedule or timetable
  • Verified trainer profiles
  • Facilities and real photo gallery
  • Location, hours and contact page
Useful features

Actions and information

  • Trial-session enquiry form
  • WhatsApp and phone actions
  • Responsive timetable layout
  • Membership comparison structure
  • Map and directions link
  • Gallery using gym-supplied photographs
Calls to action

Clear visitor next steps

Request a trial, ask about membership, view the class schedule, call or open directions.

Content responsibility

What the business must supply

The gym supplies current membership information, schedules, trainer names and verified credentials, facility details, photographs, address, timings and trial-session terms.

Relevant trust

Evidence customers can use

Current plan information, real facility photographs, verified trainer profiles, clear opening hours and straightforward trial terms. No fitness outcomes or member results should be invented.

Example scope

A realistic starting website

A typical project may include a homepage, memberships, classes, trainers, facilities and contact/location pages. Booking, member accounts and recurring payments require separate supported systems.

Concept status: This gym direction shows a possible content structure only. It does not represent paid client work, member results or a live facility.
