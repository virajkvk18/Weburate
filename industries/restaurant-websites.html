Industry website planning

A restaurant website that answers practical questions quickly.

Guests often decide on a phone shortly before visiting, reserving or ordering. A useful restaurant website keeps the menu, hours, location and next action easy to find.

Discuss Your Website Chat on WhatsApp
Customer needs

Questions the website should answer

  • Read a menu comfortably on a phone
  • Confirm opening hours, address and contact details
  • Choose between reservations, direct enquiries and delivery platforms
Recommended sections

A useful page structure

  • Homepage with food and venue direction
  • Mobile-friendly menu or linked menu document
  • Location, opening hours and contact section
  • About or dining-experience information
  • Reservation and delivery information
  • Policies or accessibility information when relevant
Useful features

Actions and information

  • Reservation link to an approved provider
  • WhatsApp or phone enquiry action
  • Map and directions link
  • Delivery-platform links
  • Food gallery using restaurant-supplied images
  • Dietary-information layout without inventing claims
Calls to action

Clear visitor next steps

Reserve a table, call the restaurant, open directions, view the menu or visit an approved delivery platform.

Content responsibility

What the business must supply

The restaurant supplies current menu items, prices, food photographs, opening hours, address, reservation process, delivery links and any dietary statements. Details must be reviewed whenever operations change.

Relevant trust

Evidence customers can use

Real food and venue images, accurate hours, consistent contact details, clear reservation expectations and approved hygiene or dietary information are more useful than generic claims.

Example scope

A realistic starting website

A typical project may include a homepage, menu, about, gallery and contact/location page, plus reservation or delivery links. Direct online ordering requires a separate supported system and is not implied.
