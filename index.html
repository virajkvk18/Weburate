About Weburate

A practical website service for smaller organisations.

Weburate helps small businesses, professionals, startups and local service providers present their work clearly online. It was created around a simple need: many capable businesses have useful services but a website that is missing, outdated or difficult to use on a phone.

Each project starts with the customer questions the website must answer. From there, Weburate agrees the scope, organises the content, designs the key journeys, develops the pages and verifies the site before launch. The guiding principles are clear communication, useful design, accessible interaction, lean code and honest claims.

Project process

Eight straightforward stages from enquiry to launch.

01

Requirement discussion

We discuss the business, audience, website goal, pages, features and preferred contact journey.

02

Scope and quotation

You receive a written scope covering deliverables, exclusions, revisions, timing and payment terms.

03

Content collection

You provide approved text, images, policies, brand files and account access needed for the work.

04

Design direction

A responsive visual direction establishes hierarchy, style and the main visitor journey.

05

Development

The approved direction becomes accessible, responsive pages with agreed forms and integrations.

06

Review and revisions

You send consolidated feedback within the revision rounds included in the written scope.

07

Testing and launch

Links, forms, devices, metadata and launch settings are checked before the approved site goes live.

08

Optional maintenance

Future updates or monitoring can be covered by a separate maintenance scope when needed.

Delivery estimates depend on timely content, access, approvals and consolidated feedback. Delays in those inputs can move the launch date.

Live client project · Bakery · Jabalpur

Manu’s Mom’s Cake

A Jabalpur home bakery presents its 100% eggless cakes, cupcakes and brownies through a mobile-first website with clear local information.

  • On-page search metadata names Vijay Nagar, Ahinsa Chowk and Jagdamba Colony alongside Jabalpur.
  • Open Graph and Twitter Card metadata provide a full preview when the site is shared.
  • The 100% eggless position is stated prominently for customers comparing bakery options.
  • The live site uses a modern JavaScript framework and is available for search indexing.
  • The supplied screens show a searchable menu with filters, a 12-step cake designer and direct order or urgent-call actions.
