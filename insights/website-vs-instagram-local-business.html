Website Versus Instagram for a Local Business

By Weburate · Drafted 17 July 2026

A practical comparison of the different jobs a business website and Instagram perform, and how local businesses can use both clearly.

Instagram is useful for discovery and updates

Instagram can show recent work, short videos, announcements and day-to-day activity. It works best when the business can publish consistently and respond to messages.

A website organises durable information

A website gives customers a stable place to find services, prices or quote boundaries, opening details, policies and contact options. The business controls the structure instead of relying on a social feed.

Search and social intent are different

Someone viewing a social post may be browsing casually. Someone searching for a specific service often wants practical information before contacting the business. Clear website pages can answer those questions.

Connect the two channels

Use Instagram to introduce the business and link to the most relevant website page. Use the website to offer a clear next action such as calling, starting a WhatsApp conversation or sending an enquiry.

Conclusion

Use the points above to prepare a clearer brief, then ask for a written scope that states deliverables, exclusions, ownership, third-party costs and support boundaries.
