How Much Does a Small-Business Website Cost in India?

By Weburate · Drafted 17 July 2026

A practical framework for understanding website cost in India without treating a generic price list as a final project quote.

Start with the business job, not a page count

A one-page campaign and a five-page service website can require very different planning. Define whether the website must explain services, collect enquiries, accept payments, publish updates or support an existing sales process.

Separate the build price from ongoing costs

Ask which expenses belong to the project and which continue after launch. Domains, hosting, paid plugins, payment-provider charges, stock assets, email services and maintenance are often billed separately.

Check what the quote includes

A useful quote states the page limit, design scope, revision rounds, responsive behaviour, contact workflow, content responsibility, integrations, delivery dependencies and post-launch support. A low headline price is difficult to compare without those details.

Allow time for content and decisions

Projects commonly slow down when service descriptions, photographs, legal policies or approvals are missing. Assign one decision-maker and prepare accurate material before the delivery estimate begins.

Conclusion

Use the points above to prepare a clearer brief, then ask for a written scope that states deliverables, exclusions, ownership, third-party costs and support boundaries.
