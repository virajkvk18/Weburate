Who it is for

When this service is useful

Small businesses, professionals, startups and local service providers that need a first website or a stronger replacement for an incomplete online presence.

Typical scope

A typical small-business project may include a homepage, about page, service pages, contact page and legal pages. The exact page limit is stated in the quote.

Problems addressed
  • The business relies only on social media or directory listings.
  • Services and contact details are difficult to understand on a phone.
  • The current online presence does not provide one reliable source of business information.
Included

What the project can cover

  • Page planning and content hierarchy
  • Responsive design for agreed page templates
  • Semantic HTML, CSS and JavaScript development
  • Contact form, phone and WhatsApp actions
  • Basic titles, descriptions, headings and indexability setup
  • Deployment and launch checks for the agreed hosting
Not included by default

Important boundaries

  • Final business copy, photography and legal policies unless quoted separately
  • Domain, hosting, paid tools and licences unless explicitly included
  • Guaranteed rankings, traffic, leads or sales
  • Unlisted dashboards, booking systems or custom applications
Process

How the work moves forward

After requirements and page scope are agreed, the client supplies approved content and brand assets. Weburate designs the main page direction, develops the approved pages, connects contact actions, tests the site and supports the agreed launch.

Price factors

What affects the quote

Page count, unique layouts, content readiness, forms, integrations, animation, editing requirements and launch access affect the quote.

What you provide

Client inputs

Approved service descriptions, business details, images, logo files, contact information, policies, examples, domain or hosting access and one person authorised to approve the work.

After launch

Support and next steps

The agreed defect-support period covers issues in delivered work. New pages, content changes, monitoring and ongoing improvements require a maintenance scope.
