One focused page for one campaign or offer.

A landing page gives a specific audience the information needed to respond to a campaign, launch, product or service without sending them through an unrelated homepage.

Who it is for

When this service is useful

Businesses running advertising, announcing an offer, validating a service, promoting an event or needing a focused lead-generation page.

Typical scope

A typical scope is one page with a clear offer, benefits, relevant proof, objections, primary CTA and enquiry mechanism.

Problems addressed
  • Campaign visitors arrive on a generic page with several competing actions.
  • The offer, audience and next step are not immediately clear.
  • Mobile users cannot scan the essential details before responding.
Included

What the project can cover

  • Audience, offer and primary-action planning
  • One responsive landing-page design
  • Lead form, phone or WhatsApp action
  • Analytics-ready event hooks for approved tools
  • Fast, accessible frontend implementation
  • Campaign-specific title, description and social metadata
Not included by default

Important boundaries

  • Advertising strategy, media buying or campaign management
  • Unapproved claims, testimonials or performance projections
  • Copywriting, photography and video production unless quoted
  • Paid analytics, CRM or automation subscriptions
Process

How the work moves forward

We confirm the traffic source, target audience, offer, proof available and one primary action. After content approval, Weburate designs, builds, connects and tests the page before it is published on the agreed domain.

Price factors

What affects the quote

Copy readiness, section count, custom illustration, forms, CRM or analytics connections, experiments and hosting constraints affect the quote.

What you provide

Client inputs

The offer, audience, approved copy, brand assets, real proof, privacy wording, desired action, campaign timing and access to the domain and measurement tools.

After launch

Support and next steps

The page can be reviewed against real campaign behaviour after launch. New experiments, copy variants and campaign updates are separate work unless included.
