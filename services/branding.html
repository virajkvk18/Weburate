Weburate service

A consistent visual identity across every customer touchpoint.

This service creates or refines your logo, colour palette, typography and basic usage guidelines so your business looks the same on your website, social accounts and printed materials.

Who it is for

When this service is useful

New businesses without an existing identity, or established businesses with an inconsistent logo, colours or fonts across different materials.

Typical scope

A typical scope includes logo design or refinement, a defined colour palette, typography selection and a short usage guide. Additional collateral such as business cards or letterheads is quoted separately.

Problems addressed
  • There is no logo, or the existing logo has no usable source files.
  • Colours and fonts differ across the website, social accounts and printed materials.
  • There are no guidelines for how the brand should be applied consistently.
Included

What the project can cover

  • Logo design or refinement with source files
  • Primary and secondary colour palette
  • Typography selection for headings and body text
  • Basic usage guidelines (do's and don'ts)
  • Social media profile and cover assets
  • One consolidated revision round
Not included by default

Important boundaries

  • Full brand strategy or naming research unless quoted separately
  • Trademark registration or legal clearance searches
  • Printed collateral production costs (design only, unless stated)
  • Unlimited logo concepts or revision rounds
Process

How the work moves forward

Weburate gathers business direction and preferences, presents logo and colour directions for feedback, refines the selected direction and delivers final files with basic guidelines.

Price factors

What affects the quote

Number of logo concepts, extent of existing brand material, additional collateral and revision rounds affect the quote.

What you provide

Client inputs

Business description, target audience, style preferences or references, and timely feedback at each review stage.

After launch

Support and next steps

Branding projects are delivered with source files and a short guide. Later extensions to the identity are quoted as new scope.
