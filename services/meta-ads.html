Weburate service

Meta Ads campaigns for Facebook and Instagram.

This service sets up and manages advertising campaigns on Facebook and Instagram, targeting an agreed audience with tracked outcomes rather than boosted posts alone.

Get a Free Consultation Chat on WhatsApp
Who it is for

When this service is useful

Businesses that want to reach a specific audience on Facebook or Instagram beyond their existing followers, with a defined budget for paid reach.

Typical scope

A typical scope includes campaign setup, audience targeting, ad creative direction, pixel or conversion API tracking and monthly optimisation. Ad spend budget is set and paid by the client directly to Meta.

Problems addressed
  • Posts are only reaching existing followers, not new potential customers.
  • Previous boosted posts had no tracking of resulting enquiries or sales.
  • Audience targeting has never been defined beyond basic location and age.
Included

What the project can cover

  • Campaign and ad set structure setup
  • Audience targeting and audience testing
  • Ad creative direction using supplied or created assets
  • Meta Pixel or Conversions API setup
  • Monthly optimisation within the agreed budget
  • Monthly performance report
Not included by default

Important boundaries

  • Meta ad spend budget, which is paid separately by the client
  • Guaranteed cost-per-result, reach or sales figures
  • Original photography or video production unless quoted separately
  • Account recovery from prior policy restrictions without separate assessment
Process

How the work moves forward

Weburate agrees the goal, audience and budget, sets up campaigns and tracking, then reviews creative and targeting performance on an agreed monthly cycle.

Price factors

What affects the quote

Number of campaigns, creative production needs, tracking complexity and reporting frequency affect the management fee, which is separate from ad spend.

What you provide

Client inputs

Meta Business Suite access, monthly ad spend budget, brand or product assets, and approval of ad creative before launch.

After launch

Support and next steps

Campaign management is billed monthly. Ad spend budget is controlled and paid directly by the client at all times.
