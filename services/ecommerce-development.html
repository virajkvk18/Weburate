Weburate service

An online store planned around products and fulfilment.

This service structures a product catalogue, shopping journey and checkout using an agreed commerce platform and an approved third-party payment provider.

Who it is for

When this service is useful

Businesses with prepared product information, clear pricing, fulfilment rules and the operational capacity to manage orders after launch.

Typical scope

A typical initial store includes agreed collections, product templates, cart, provider-hosted payment flow, core policy pages and an order-management handover.

Problems addressed
  • Products are hard to browse or compare on mobile.
  • Shipping, returns, taxes or payment information appears too late.
  • Product data is inconsistent and store operations are not defined.
Included

What the project can cover

  • Platform and catalogue planning
  • Collection, product, cart and checkout configuration
  • Responsive storefront design within platform capabilities
  • Approved payment-provider integration
  • Shipping, tax and notification configuration when supported
  • Policy-page structure and store handover
Not included by default

Important boundaries

  • Payment-provider approval, PCI obligations handled by the provider or merchant
  • Product photography, legal advice or policy drafting
  • Unlimited product entry, marketplace operations or order fulfilment
  • Custom platform functionality not listed in the quote
Process

How the work moves forward

Before design, we clarify product count, variants, inventory, tax, shipping, returns, payments and who owns product data. The selected platform is configured, products within scope are added, checkout is tested and the owner receives an operations handover.

Price factors

What affects the quote

Platform choice, catalogue size, variants, product entry, shipping logic, taxes, payment providers, custom theme work and integrations affect the quote.

What you provide

Client inputs

Accurate product names, descriptions, prices, images, stock information, variants, tax rules, shipping rules, policies, legal business details and provider accounts.

After launch

Support and next steps

The owner manages products, orders, refunds and fulfilment unless a separate support agreement says otherwise. Platform fees, updates and maintenance continue after launch.
