Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what information Weburate collects through this website, why we collect it, and how it is used when you get in touch about a project.

What we collect

When you submit the project enquiry form or contact us directly, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, email, phone number, business name and a description of your project. The enquiry form on this site sends this information directly to our email inbox as an email you send from your own device — it is not stored on a server or database.

How we use it

We use the information you share only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and communicate with you about your project. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. If that changes, this policy will be updated to reflect it.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy or want any information you have shared with us removed, email weburateinfotech@gmail.com or message us on WhatsApp or Instagram.
