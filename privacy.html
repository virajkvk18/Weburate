Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 July 2026

This policy explains how Weburate handles information shared through this website. It is a practical description, not legal advice.

Information collected

The project form may collect your name, email address, optional phone number, optional business name, selected website type, optional budget range and project description. Direct email, phone, WhatsApp or Instagram conversations contain whatever information you choose to share.

Why it is collected

Weburate uses enquiry information only to understand the request, respond, prepare a quote and communicate about a possible or active project. Form-field values are not sent to analytics tools or used for unrelated marketing.

How form information is delivered

The form sends information to a Vercel serverless function. When configured, that function passes the enquiry to Resend, an email delivery provider, so it can reach Weburate’s configured inbox. The website does not intentionally store form submissions in its own database.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity are optional and load only when their public IDs are added to the site configuration. If enabled, those providers may process device, interaction and approximate location data under their own policies. Weburate should assess whether consent is required for the visitors and markets it serves before enabling either tool.

Retention and sharing

Enquiries may remain in the receiving email account while Weburate responds, prepares or manages work, meets legitimate record-keeping needs or resolves disputes. Enquiry records should be reviewed and deleted when they are no longer reasonably needed for those purposes, subject to legal or contractual requirements. Information is not sold. It may be processed by hosting, email and analytics providers only as described above or when required by law.

Your choices

You may ask what enquiry information is held or request deletion, subject to legitimate legal or record-keeping needs, by emailing weburateinfotech@gmail.com.

Security and updates

Reasonable technical controls reduce risk, but no internet transmission is guaranteed completely secure. This page will be updated when material handling practices change.
